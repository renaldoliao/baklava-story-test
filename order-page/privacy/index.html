Baklava Story

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Baklava Story is a bakery in the Mission District of San Francisco. This page explains what we collect when you place a pickup order on this website, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your order: the trays you chose, your pickup day and time, and any note you add.

We do not collect payment details. You pay when you pick up.

How we use it

  • To prepare and hand over your order.
  • If you tick the box to get texts: to text you about that order, a confirmation with your order details and pickup time, and a message when it is ready.
  • To contact you if there is a problem with your order.

Text messages

We text the phone number you enter only if you tick the box to get texts, and only about the order you placed. We do not send marketing texts. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

You can reply STOP to any text to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Who handles your information

We do not sell your information. We use a few services to run the shop, and they process it on our behalf only for that purpose: Google (Sheets and Apps Script, to keep our order list and send texts) and Telnyx (to deliver the text messages). Your information is also visible to the staff who prepare orders.

How long we keep it

We keep order records so we can look up past orders and plan baking. You can ask us to delete your name and phone number at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we have about you, message us on Instagram @baklava.story.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
